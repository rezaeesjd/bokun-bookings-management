# Version 1.4.2 — Operating profit + monthly run-rate in Business Results

The **Business Results** metric view (Analytics tab) now carries the P&L all
the way to the bottom line instead of stopping at net margin. Previously the
final-after-overhead number and the monthly figures lived only behind the
"View full overhead P&L →" drill-down; they're now shown inline.

## What's new in the view

- **Operating profit tile** — added alongside the existing tiles (Bookings,
  Catalog-matched, Coverage, Gross earned, Partner cost, Net margin, Avg
  margin). It's the filtered set's net margin less overhead for the months that
  set covers, and turns red when negative.
- **Waterfall extended** — the Gross → − Partner cost → = Net margin bars now
  continue with **− Overhead** and **= Operating profit**, so the whole chain is
  visible in one place.
- **Per-month run-rate strip** — a new section shows **Months covered**, **Avg
  monthly margin**, **Monthly overhead**, and **Monthly operating profit**, with
  a note spelling out how period overhead is derived
  (`monthly overhead × months`).

## Basis (unchanged model)

- Overhead stays **business-wide and fixed per month** — it is *not* reduced by
  the sidebar/date filters, matching the earlier decision and the full P&L.
  The note under the monthly strip says so explicitly.
- The month count is the distinct creation months of the filtered, in-currency
  rows — the same basis the full P&L uses — so one booking's month still counts
  as one month and an operating-profit figure never divides by zero.
- Everything remains in the single most-common currency of the filtered set;
  money is never summed across currencies.
- No data-model or net-revenue formula change; this only surfaces the overhead
  subtraction (already computed in the full P&L) inside the filter-driven view.

## Tests

Analytics interaction suite now **23 checks** — added a guard asserting the
operating-profit subtraction and the monthly run-rate (two matched bookings
across two months with 100/month overhead → net margin 120, operating profit
−80, monthly operating profit −40). Full suite green.
